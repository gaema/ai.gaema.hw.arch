INT8 — throughput across parts

Vector and matrix-engine throughput for this operand, on the parts whose vendors publish a rate for it.

The most widely published operand of the set: two of the six architectures quote a whole-part INT8 matrix rate, and it is the format on which the Intel parts are quoted at all — Xe2 has no 8-bit float matrix path, so low precision on those parts means integer.

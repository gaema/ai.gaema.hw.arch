4-bit float — throughput across parts

Vector and matrix-engine throughput for this operand, on the parts whose vendors publish a rate for it.

Only one architecture here has a 4-bit FLOAT matrix path, and its published rates are the largest numbers on this site. They are also the most easily misread: both figures NVIDIA publishes with a basis are 2:4-SPARSE, by its own footnote, so the dense chart on this page is empty and the sparse one is not. NVIDIA quotes a single 4-bit-float rate and does not break it out by encoding, so both the FP4 E2M1 and the NVFP4 rows of the format table point here.

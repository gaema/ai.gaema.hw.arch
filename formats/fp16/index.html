FP16 — throughput across parts

Vector and matrix-engine throughput for this operand, on the parts whose vendors publish a rate for it.

The one operand on this site with both a published vector rate and a published matrix rate on the same part, which is what makes it the clearest illustration of the gap between the two classes. It is also where the accumulate axis bites hardest: two of the five architectures here that own a matrix engine will add an FP16 product into either FP16 or FP32, and not one of the published rates says which it is for.

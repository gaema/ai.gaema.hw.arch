Six AI accelerators, drawn the same way

Every vendor names its parts differently — a compute unit, an Xe-core, a streaming multiprocessor, a Tensix tile — and every vendor draws its own block diagram at its own level of detail. These six are drawn to one structure, so you can click from the card down to the thing that actually does the multiply and compare like with like.

How to read these diagrams

Each page opens at the card and descends: click any block that says open › to go inside it, use the breadcrumb to come back out, and press Esc to go up one level. The panel on the right carries the specs for wherever you are. Every view has its own URL, so a link can point straight at, say, a single SIMD32 inside a compute unit.

The diagrams are logical, not floorplans. Blocks are arranged for legibility, not to physical scale or position, and a harvested part is drawn as the full die with the disabled units marked — no vendor publishes which specific units a given card has fused off. Figures come only from published vendor documentation and press coverage; each page lists its sources.

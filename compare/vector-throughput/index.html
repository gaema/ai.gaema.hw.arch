The lanes, not the matrix engine

What the general-purpose SIMD lanes do: the rate that applies to elementwise work, normalisation, activation, and to any matrix multiply whose shape the matrix engine will not take. It is the smaller number on every part here by roughly an order of magnitude, which is the reason it is worth its own page — a kernel that falls off the matrix path lands on this chart, not on the other one.

Every figure is a published peak — a ceiling, not a measurement. No performance measurement of ours appears here.

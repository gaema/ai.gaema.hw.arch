Matrix engines, compared by what they publish

Twelve of the thirteen parts have a matrix engine. The Radeon 610M does not. Of the twelve, the Arc A370M and the Blackhole p300c publish no peak. The other ten quote one, almost never for the same format and never at the same basis, and two of those quotes are only for the sparse case. This page puts each published rate beside the operand it was quoted for, the clock it was quoted at, and whether it is dense or 2:4-sparse — one chart per basis, because a chart that mixed them would rank these parts by which vendor quotes which number.

Every figure is a published peak — a ceiling, not a measurement. No performance measurement of ours appears here.
